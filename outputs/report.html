Method and limitations

Seed 42; 12,020 raw rows; 12,000 original devices; 100 devices without a valid result. Invalid rows are excluded and disclosed, not silently counted as passing. Missing results can bias observed yield.

Each device has one assigned test condition. Temperature groups contain different devices. Yield here is not proof of passing every operating condition. Limits: leakage ≤ 10 µA, current ≤ 60 mA, gain ≥ 18 dB. Gain and leakage are illustrative numerical models, not a validated RF/device simulator. No predictive ML is implemented.
